      Slice City
      WELCOME TO THE COUNTER

      How to play

      1. Read the order and tap ingredients to build a pizza.
      2. Bake, then serve to get paid. Remake anytime before serving.
      3. Accurate orders earn tips. Perfect streaks earn extra cash.
      4. Serve unlimited customers between 8 AM and 6 PM. Five game minutes pass every two seconds. A full day lasts four real minutes.
      5. Hire workers and let them prepare, bake, and sell pizzas on autopilot, or take control to cook yourself.
      6. Open the City Map to buy and visit shops. Each has its own kitchen and equipment.
      7. Hire named workers and assign them on the Crew screen. They make and sell pizzas at every staffed shop from 8 AM to 6 PM.
      8. From day 6, each day has a new special and a cash-bonus challenge.

      The timer starts with your first ingredient: 75 seconds, gradually reducing to 60. Remaking keeps the timer running. Map and crew screens keep the day running. Pause whenever you need; popup menus and hidden browser tabs freeze the game.
